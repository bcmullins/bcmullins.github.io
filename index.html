I am an Assistant Professor of Computer and Information Science at Allegheny College working on trustworthy computing and statistical data privacy. I completed my PhD in computer science at UMass Amherst, where I worked with Dan Sheldon and Gerome Miklau, and was a Visiting Professor at Mount Holyoke College.

Before this, I was a Data Scientist in industry and a researcher at the Fiscal Research Center, a public policy think tank in Atlanta. I studied mathematics & logic at Miami University and philosophy & economics at Georgia State University.

Research Interests

Differential Privacy, Explainable Machine Learning, Synthetic Data,
